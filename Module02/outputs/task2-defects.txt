# Defect 3

part_no | unit_price
P-1001  | 12.50

**Numeric comparison for contrast** 
SELECT part_no, unit_price
FROM part
WHERE CAST(unit_price AS REAL) < 15;

- Result: 
part_no | unit_price
P-1001  | 12.50
P-1003  | 9.90
P-1003  | 9.90

- Text ordering excludes the 9.90 parts from a “prices below 15” query, which can lead to incorrect purchasing or invoice calculations.
